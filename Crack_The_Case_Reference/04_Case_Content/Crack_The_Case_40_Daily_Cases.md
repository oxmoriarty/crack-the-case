## CASE #1 — The Missing Master Key
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Incident:** A master key to the community archive vanished from a locked reception drawer during a short afternoon handover. No lock was damaged, and only three staff members had access.

### Suspects
- **Mara Venn — Receptionist:** “I placed the key in the drawer at 1:00 p.m. and did not open it again. I was at the front desk until the alarm sounded.”
- **Ivo Grant — Archivist:** “I borrowed the key at noon to open the archive, returned it before lunch, and went straight to the records room.”
- **Nell Park — Cleaner:** “I cleaned reception from 1:10 to 1:25. I did not touch the drawer; I used my own supply-cabinet key.”

### Clues

1. **Drawer log:** The electronic drawer log records one opening at 1:18 p.m., using Mara's staff code.
2. **Reception rota:** Mara's rota shows she was assigned to the front desk, but the desk camera still image at 1:18 shows the chair empty.
3. **Key tag:** A small strip of blue thread matching Mara's cardigan is caught in the drawer handle.

### Hint
Who had the drawer opened, and whose account does the log identify?

### Authoritative answer key
- **Culprit:** Mara Venn
- **Decisive clue:** Drawer log
- **Solution explanation:** Mara used her own code to open the drawer while briefly leaving the desk. The thread is corroborating, but the access log is decisive.
- **Deduction check:** Mara claims she never reopened the drawer, but the authenticated log records her code opening it during the disappearance window.
