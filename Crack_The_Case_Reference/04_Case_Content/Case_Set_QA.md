# Case Set QA Summary

Total cases: 40

## Editorial review requirement
These are a complete first authored set. Before production, run an editorial playtest for clarity, mobile text length, clue strength, and unintended alternate interpretations. The answer key is authoritative; any revision must preserve unique solvability.

## Difficulty intent
- Easy: direct contradiction or access/timing deduction, but still requires comparing at least two pieces of information.
- Standard: multiple clues reinforce a contradiction, establish a sequence, or distinguish authorship from access/approval.
- Challenging: connected deductions, timeline reconciliation, indirect access, or linked records; no arbitrary leaps or specialist knowledge.
